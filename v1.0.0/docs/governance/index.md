# Governance

> Licensing, trademark, the pre-launch legal assessment, and per-asset provenance.

---

LLMS index: [llms.txt](/brand/v1.0.0/llms.txt)

---

This section states what you may and may not do with the material in this
repository. It is deliberately explicit: a design system that is
source-available without clear terms is a liability for everyone who touches it.

---

Section pages:

- [Licensing](/brand/v1.0.0/docs/governance/licensing/): A split licence — brand assets are proprietary, code and tokens are MIT.
- [Trademark](/brand/v1.0.0/docs/governance/trademark/): Guidelines for referring to projectious.work and using its marks.
- [Legal assessment](/brand/v1.0.0/docs/governance/legal-assessment/): Pre-launch clearance review of the name, mark, fonts, icons, and imagery sources.
- [Asset provenance](/brand/v1.0.0/docs/governance/provenance/): Per-asset source, licence, and attribution status for every third-party dependency.
