All three fonts are SIL OFL 1.1 — the most permissive open font license. 4. Icon library: Lucide
License verification for commercial use
Lucide Icons
Low risk
Lucide is licensed under the ISC License (functionally equivalent to MIT). This permits: free commercial use, modification, redistribution, sublicensing. No attribution required in the product UI (only in source code). Over 1,400 icons, actively maintained. Fork of Feather Icons with broader community. The ISC license is one of the most permissive open-source licenses available.
