Slide deck rules
Structure: Title → Section divider → Content slides → Closing. Section dividers between major topics.
Typography: Maximum two type sizes per slide. Headings in Plus Jakarta Sans, body in Source Sans 3. Code in IBM Plex Mono on midnight-2 background.
Color: Dark slides (title, divider, closing) use midnight-dark. Content slides use white. Never mix — a slide is either dark or light, not both (except the image+text split).
Spacing: 20px padding all sides. Bottom footer with logo (left) and page number (right) on content slides only.
Imagery: No clip art, no stock photos, no decorative gradients. Diagrams use the illustration style (abstract system diagrams). Photos follow the photography guidelines.
Progress dots: Title slide only. Three dots, first in accent, rest translucent.
