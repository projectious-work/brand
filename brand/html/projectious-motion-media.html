Motion rules
Things slide into place, they don't bounce. All animation wraps in @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference). CSS-only for HTML. For React, use Framer Motion with the same timing values. Never animate text character-by-character. Page transitions: 400ms max.

Planned medium — sonic mood defined for consistency from day one

Podcast format
Cover art: midnight background, wordmark in white, accent element. Episode cards: consistent template — title in Plus Jakarta Sans on midnight or white. Transcripts published alongside every episode, styled with the documentation patterns. All sounds optional, respect system mute.
Background music direction
Ambient, unobtrusive, mid-tempo electronic with warmth. No lyrics. No corporate ukulele. No cyberpunk synth. Think: Tycho, Boards of Canada-adjacent. Music fades under speech within 0.5s. Max volume when present: -18dB relative to voice.

Video rules
Demos at real-time speed, no fast-forward. Transitions: cut or 200ms fade — no swoops. All video uses midnight surfaces. Subtitles always available. Export at 1080p minimum, 4K when possible.
Photography direction
Candid, European/international, warm grading, no stock clichés
Do
Real workspace environments. Candid, mid-shot or detail shots (hands on keyboards, screens with real interfaces). Desaturated to harmonize with palette. Warm color grading. European/international feel — location-agnostic, not US-centric.
Don't
Staged environments. Posed-looking-at-camera corporate headshots. Stock photo people pointing at whiteboards. Neon-lit "hacker" aesthetics. Overly saturated colors. Generic open-plan office shots. AI-generated faces.
Treatment
Desaturate 15–25%. Apply warm color grade (shift shadows toward midnight, highlights toward slate-2). Reduce contrast slightly. If used on dark backgrounds, apply midnight overlay at 20–40% opacity. Never use photos as full-bleed backgrounds for text.

Photography sources
Preferred: Unsplash, Pexels (royalty-free, no attribution required for commercial). Custom photography when possible — commission from local European photographers. Never use Shutterstock/Getty watermarked or AI-generated imagery. All photos must pass the "would a developer trust this?" test.
Presentation animation
Rules for slide transitions and element animation in decks and demos
Slide transitions
Cut or 200ms fade only. No swoops, wipes, flips, or 3D rotations. Between major sections, a brief fade-through-midnight is acceptable (fade to midnight 200ms, hold 100ms, fade to next slide 200ms).
Element animation
Staggered fade-up: 100ms delay between items, 300ms per item. Charts: draw-in from left over 400ms. Never animate text character-by-character. Never auto-advance slides. Demos at real-time speed.
