# 🛡️ 2. Trademark-style risk analysis

## Distinctiveness (very important)
“Projectious” is:
- Derived from “project” + “-ious”
- Not a dictionary word  
- Slightly descriptive *in concept* (projects), but **not directly descriptive of consulting services**

According to trademark principles:
- Strong marks are **invented or suggestive**  
- Descriptive ones are weaker

👉 “Projectious” sits in the **suggestive / semi-invented zone** → generally registrable

---

## Likelihood of conflict
Trademark law focuses on **consumer confusion**  

Potential overlaps:
- Words like “project management,” “project consulting”
- Names starting with “Project…”

But:
- The suffix **“-ious” creates enough phonetic and visual distinction**
- It’s not just “Project Consulting GmbH” (which would be weak)

👉 **Risk level: LOW to LOW-MODERATE** (still quite safe)

---

## Important nuance
Even if:
- Someone has “Project X Consulting”

That usually **does NOT block you**, unless:
- The name is *very similar* AND  
- You operate in the same niche AND  
- Confusion is likely

👉 Small differences are often enough to avoid conflict in company names
