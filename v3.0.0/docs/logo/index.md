# Logo

> The mark, the four lockups, and the rules that keep them legible.


The mark is a stencil peony bud — concentric petal cuts resolving to an accent
centre. It reads as a single form at 16px and holds its internal structure at
poster scale.


- [Lockups](https://projectious-work.github.io/brand/v3.0.0/docs/logo/lockups/index.md) — Four approved lockups, rendered live, and where each one belongs.
- [Usage](https://projectious-work.github.io/brand/v3.0.0/docs/logo/usage/index.md) — Clear space, minimum sizes, monochrome variants, and placing the mark on colour and photography.
- [Files](https://projectious-work.github.io/brand/v3.0.0/docs/logo/files/index.md) — The canonical SVG, raster, and favicon assets in design system v2.1.1.
